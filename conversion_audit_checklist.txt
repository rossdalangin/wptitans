# TITAN AUTHORITY & CONVERSION AUDIT CHECKLIST
## Use this 30-point checklist to perform "Free Audits" for high-ticket prospects.

---

### 1. THE "3-SECOND RULE" (FIRST IMPRESSION)
[ ] Does the H1 clearly state WHAT they do and WHO they serve?
[ ] Is the "Vibe" professional or amateur (fonts/colors)?
[ ] Is there a clear primary CTA in the top right of the header?
[ ] Does the hero image/video support the claim or distract from it?
[ ] Is the site SSL-secured and fast-loading (under 2s)?

---

### 2. TRUST & SOCIAL PROOF (AUTHORITY GAP)
[ ] Is there a "As Seen In" or "Clients Served" trust bar above the fold?
[ ] Are testimonials specific (results-based) rather than generic ("great guy")?
[ ] Are there high-quality logos of recognizable brands?
[ ] Is there a "Founded by" or "Meet the Team" section to humanize the brand?
[ ] Are there visible case study links or a "Portfolio" button?

---

### 3. USER EXPERIENCE (FRICTION REDUCTION)
[ ] Is the navigation menu simple (max 5-7 items)?
[ ] Are buttons "Magnetic" or do they have clear hover states?
[ ] Is the typography readable (min 16px for body)?
[ ] Does the site use whitespace effectively or is it cluttered?
[ ] Are there broken links or 404s?

---

### 4. MOBILE OPTIMIZATION (THE "THUMB" TEST)
[ ] Are buttons large enough to tap easily?
[ ] Does the content stack logically on mobile?
[ ] Is there a "Sticky" CTA button for mobile users?
[ ] Do images scale correctly or overlap text?
[ ] Is the mobile menu (hamburger) easy to find and use?

---

### 5. CONVERSION ARCHITECTURE (LEAD GEN)
[ ] Is there an "Exit-Intent" lead magnet (PDF, Guide, Audit)?
[ ] Are forms simple (max 4 fields for initial contact)?
[ ] Does every page end with a "Final Call to Action"?
[ ] Is there a "Project Planner" or interactive tool for high-intent leads?
[ ] Are "Trust Seals" (Guarantee, Secure Pay, Award) placed near CTAs?

---

### 6. MESSAGING & PSYCHOLOGY
[ ] Does the copy use "You" (customer-centric) more than "We" (company-centric)?
[ ] Is the problem the customer is facing clearly articulated?
[ ] Is the "14-Day Transformation" (or specific timeline) mentioned?
[ ] Are there clear "Pain Point" headers?
[ ] Is there a sense of scarcity or exclusivity ("Only accepting 2 clients this month")?

---

**AUDIT SCORECARD:**
- 0-10: Critical (Total Rebuild Needed)
- 11-20: Underperforming (Major Conversion Optimization Needed)
- 21-30: High Performer (Minor Polishing Needed)
