# AGENCY HIRING & SCALING GUIDE
## Building the Titan Core Team

---

### 1. THE FIRST HIRE: PROJECT MANAGER (Operations)
**Role**: Shield the founder from day-to-day client communication and coordinate the 14-day sprint.
**Key Skill**: Extreme organization and high-level empathy.

### 2. THE SECOND HIRE: JUNIOR DEVELOPER / WP SPECIALIST
**Role**: Handle site setup, content population, and Customizer configurations.
**Key Skill**: Proficiency in WordPress, CSS, and attention to detail.

### 3. THE THIRD HIRE: CONVERSION COPYWRITER (Contractor)
**Role**: Draft the Authority Blueprints and high-converting page copy.
**Key Skill**: Direct-response marketing and brand voice development.

### 4. THE FOURTH HIRE: APPOINTMENT SETTER / SDR
**Role**: Manage outbound LinkedIn outreach and follow up on "Free Audit" leads.
**Key Skill**: Persistence and ability to identify high-value prospects.

---

### INTERVIEW SCORECARD CRITERIA:
1. **Speed**: Can they deliver quality work under pressure?
2. **Authority**: Do they present themselves with professional confidence?
3. **Systems-Thinking**: Do they follow SOPs or try to "reinvent the wheel"?
4. **Growth Mindset**: Are they looking to build something world-class?

---

### ONBOARDING PROCESS:
- **Week 1**: Deep dive into the Titan Theme and Customizer Logic.
- **Week 2**: Shadow a full 14-Day Sprint.
- **Week 3**: Lead a specific section of the project (e.g., Development).
- **Week 4**: Full project ownership under Founder supervision.
