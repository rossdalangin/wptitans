# CLIENT CASE STUDY INTERVIEW GUIDE
## Extracting High-Ticket Results for the Titan Portfolio.

---

### 1. THE CHALLENGE (The Origin)
- "What was the single biggest problem you were facing before we started?"
- "What did your old website look like, and how did it make you feel when sending it to prospects?"
- "Had you tried other agencies before? If so, what was the outcome?"

### 2. THE TRANSFORMATION (The System)
- "What were your initial thoughts when you heard about our 14-day Authority Sprint?"
- "What part of our process (Strategy, Design, Performance) had the biggest impact on you?"
- "How would you describe the 'new feel' of your brand to a colleague?"

### 3. THE RESULTS (The ROI)
- "Have you noticed a change in the quality or quantity of leads since launch?"
- "What specific feedback have you received from your clients or peers?"
- "What is the approximate ROI you’ve seen (or expect to see) from this rebrand?"

### 4. THE RECOMMENDATION (The Hook)
- "What would you say to an expert who is currently on the fence about working with us?"
- "If you could summarize the experience in one sentence, what would it be?"

---
*Pro Tip: Record the interview via Zoom. Use the transcript to write the 'Challenge' and 'Transformation' sections, and use the video clips for the Titan social feed.*
