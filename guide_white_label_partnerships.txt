# SCALING THROUGH WHITE-LABEL PARTNERSHIPS
## How to Scale Your Agency Revenue without Increasing Payroll.

---

### 1. THE WHITE-LABEL MODEL
In layman's terms: You sell the high-ticket "Titan" system under your brand, but another expert team (the white-label partner) does the heavy lifting. This allows you to focus 100% on sales and strategy.

### 2. WHEN TO USE A PARTNER
- **Capacity Overload**: You have more 14-day sprint requests than your internal team can handle.
- **Niche Expansion**: You want to offer services like "Advanced App Development" that are outside your core expertise.
- **Margin Optimization**: A partner may have lower COGS, allowing you to maintain 60%+ margins.

### 3. HOW TO VET AN ELITE PARTNER
[ ] **Quality Check**: Do their past projects meet the Titan Signature standard ( sub-2s speed, cinematic design)?
[ ] **Process Alignment**: Can they follow your 14-day sprint SOP exactly?
[ ] **Communication**: Do they respond within 4 hours during the work day?
[ ] **Discretion**: Are they willing to sign an NDA and remain "invisible" to your client?

### 4. THE FINANCIAL ARCHITECTURE
- **Your Retail Price**: $6,500 (Authority System).
- **Partner Cost**: $2,500 - $3,500.
- **Your Net Profit**: $3,000+ for strategy and management only.

### 5. MANAGING THE PARTNERSHIP
- Provide them with the **Project Discovery Brief** on Day 1.
- They upload drafts directly to your **Shared Drive**.
- You record the Loom walkthrough for the client. The client never knows the partner exists.

---
*Scale through systems, not just headcounts.*
