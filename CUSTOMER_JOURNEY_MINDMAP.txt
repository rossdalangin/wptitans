CORE CONVERSION LOGIC
- No Dead Ends: Every page must end with a CTA or a link to a resource.
- Authority First: All deliverables (PDFs/Videos) must use premium brand terminology.
- Frictionless: Forms should be multi-step or AJAX to keep the user engaged.
- Automation: Emails must trigger immediately to capitalize on dopamine spikes.
