DETAILED JOURNEY PHASES
-----------------------

PHASE 1: THE INITIATION (Entry & Awareness)
- Entry Points: Authority LP, ROI Calculator, Project Planner, or Knowledge Vault.
- User Sees: High-fidelity, cinematic branding. They realize their "Authority Gap."
- User Action: Fills a multi-step form (Frictionless initiation).
- Success State: Redirects to /thank-you-initialization/.

PHASE 2: THE QUALIFICATION (Value & Trust)
- User Receives: "The Authority Pack" (PDF) + Confirmation Email.
- Interaction: If they requested an audit, they receive a manual video scan within 24hrs.
- Objective: Move the user from "Interested" to "Booked Call."

PHASE 3: THE CONVERSION (High-Stakes Sales)
- Action: Attend Strategy Consultation.
- Deliverable: "The Velocity Roadmap" (Custom project plan).
- The Signing: Client receives links to:
  1. Master Services Agreement (MSA) - Digital Sign.
  2. Stripe Payment Link (Deposit).
- Transition: Once paid, redirect to /success-onboarding-complete/.

PHASE 4: THE ACCELERATION (14-Day Velocity Sprint)
- Action: Client completes Onboarding Questionnaire.
- User Access: Immediate login to "Client Command Center" (Portal).
- Milestones: Strategy (Day 3) --> Design (Day 7) --> Dev (Day 12) --> Launch (Day 14).
- Visibility: Client sees real-time progress on their dashboard.

PHASE 5: THE LEGACY (Retention & Advocacy)
- Post-Launch: 30 days of "Hyper-Support."
- Results Review: Quarterly audit of performance metrics.
- Expansion: Upsell to "Titan Maintenance" or "LinkedIn Authority Engine."
- Advocacy: Testimonial and referral program initiation.


CORE LOGIC: THE "STICK" FACTOR
At every stage, the user receives an asset (PDF, Video, Dashboard access) that reinforces the agency's premium status. This makes it socially and professionally difficult for them to "churn" or choose a cheaper competitor.
