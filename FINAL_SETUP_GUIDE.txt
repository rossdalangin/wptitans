TITAN AGENCY: FINAL SETUP & EXECUTION GUIDE
===========================================

This guide explains how to connect your pages, forms, and strategic assets to execute the conversion protocols defined in the MINDMAP.

STEP 1: INITIAL THEME DEPLOYMENT
- Navigate to Appearance > Customize > Theme Setup.
- Check "Generate Essential Pages" and hit Publish.
- Result: Your full sitemap (27 pages) is created.

STEP 2: CONNECTING THE CONVERSION PATHS
- Go to Customizer > Lead Generation & Modals.
  - Set "Audit Success Redirect" to your /thank-you-initialization/ page.
  - Set "Exit Intent Redirect" to your /resource-center/ page.
- Go to Customizer > 17. Contact Strategy.
  - Set "Contact Success Redirect" to your /thank-you-initialization/ page.

STEP 3: THE HIGH-TICKET REDIRECT (PROJECT PLANNER)
- The Project Planner automatically redirects leads with budgets > $5,000 to the Book a Strategy Call page.
- You can change this URL in Customizer > 17. Contact Strategy > "High-Value Lead Redirect URL."

STEP 4: ASSET FULFILLMENT
- Use an automation tool (Zapier, Make, or WP Mail SMTP) to trigger emails based on form submissions.
- Map the following assets from the root directory to your emails:
  1. Post-Booking Confirmation --> Send "Pre-Call Authority Pack" (PDF).
  2. Audit Request --> Deliver "3-Point Authority Protocol" (Video/PDF).
  3. Exit Intent --> Send "The $100k Authority Blueprint" (PDF).

STEP 5: CLIENT PORTAL SETUP
- Once a project begins, provide the client with the login details for /client-portal/.
- Update the shared links (MSA, Drive, Roadmap) in Customizer > Client Portal Settings.
