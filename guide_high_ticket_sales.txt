# THE TITAN SALES FRAMEWORK: CLOSING HIGH-TICKET DEALS
## A Step-by-Step Script and Psychology Guide for 14-Day Sprint Signs.

---

### THE PSYCHOLOGY: FROM "VENDOR" TO "COMMANDER"
Most agency owners act like "order takers."
- **Order Taker**: "What do you want your site to look like?"
- **The Commander**: "Based on your goals, your current site is failing you here, here, and here. Here is how we will fix it."

---

### PHASE 1: THE 15-MINUTE DISCOVERY SCRIPT (Word-for-Word)

**1. THE ANCHOR (0-3 Mins)**
"I’ve spent some time reviewing your current platform and the ROI Calculator results you submitted. Before we dive in, tell me—what is the #1 business goal that your current website is actually *preventing* you from hitting right now?"

**2. THE AUTHORITY GAP (3-8 Mins)**
"I see. You are clearly an expert at [What they do], but looking at the site, there is a massive 'Authority Gap.' To a prospect who doesn't know you, you look like a beginner. Does that resonate?"
*(Wait for them to agree. This is where the sale is won.)*

**3. THE TRANSFORMATION (8-12 Mins)**
"We don't do 'standard' web design. We deploy the **Authority Website System™.** It’s a 14-day intensive sprint where we handle the strategy, the high-converting copy, and the high-performance design. We take the burden off you so you can stay in your zone of genius."

**4. THE CLOSE (12-15 Mins)**
"We have two slots left for our next sprint starting [Date]. The investment is $[Amount]. If we don't hit our 14-day deadline, you get a 50% discount. Are you ready to weaponize your expertise, or do you want to keep losing leads to that gap?"

---

### PHASE 2: HANDLING THE "IT'S TOO EXPENSIVE" OBJECTION
In layman's terms: They are afraid. Not of the price, but of failing.
- **Titan Response**: "I understand the investment feels significant. But let’s look at your LTV (Lifetime Value). If this system secures just ONE additional high-ticket client for [Company Name], the entire project has paid for itself and then some. Does it make sense to keep saving a few thousand dollars while losing tens of thousands in revenue?"

---

### PHASE 3: THE SIGNATURE "DEPOSIT" RULE
Never leave a call with "Let me think about it."
- **Action**: "I'll send over the Master Services Agreement (MSA) now. Once the 50% deposit is secured, your 14-day clock officially begins. I'll see you on our Phase 1 Blueprint call."

---
*Command the Room. Secure the Bag.*
