# THE TITAN DISCOVERY SCRIPT: HIGH-TICKET PERSUASION
## 15-Minute Strategic Audit to Close $5k+ Engagements.

---

### STAGE 1: SETTING THE FRAME (0:00 - 2:00)
"Hi [Name], thanks for jumping on. I’ve reviewed your [Company Name] site and the ROI data you sent over. The goal of this call is simple: I want to see if your current digital presence is a liability or an asset, and if we are the right team to fix it. Sound good?"
*Psychology: Authority framing. You are the expert reviewing them, not a vendor begging for work.*

---

### STAGE 2: THE PAIN DIAGNOSIS (2:00 - 7:00)
"Tell me, when a high-value prospect lands on your current site, what is the #1 reason they might decide NOT to work with you?"
*(Wait for answer)*
"I noticed your current load time is [X] seconds and the messaging is a bit generic. In layman's terms: You are an expert business with a 'starter kit' website. That **Authority Gap** is costing you [Reference ROI Calculator Amount] every single month. How much longer can you afford that leak?"

---

### STAGE 3: THE AUTHORITY TRANSFORMATION (7:00 - 11:00)
"We solve this with the **14-Day Authority Sprint.** We don't just 'design'—we weaponize your expertise. We handle the copywriting blueprint, the cinematic design, and the high-performance WordPress build. Most importantly, we provide a **14-Day Delivery Guarantee.** If we aren't ready for your review in two weeks, the project is 50% off. We take the risk, because our system works."

---

### STAGE 4: ANCHORING & THE CLOSE (11:00 - 15:00)
"A system of this caliber typically takes 4-6 months with a traditional agency. We’ve compressed that into 14 days of high-intensity work. The investment for the flagship Authority System is $[Price].

I have one slot left for our [Date] sprint. To secure it, we require a 50% deposit and a signed Master Services Agreement. Based on our conversation, do you feel [Company Name] is ready to command the authority it deserves?"

---
*PRO TIP: If they hesitate, lean into 'Loss Aversion'. Remind them of the revenue they are losing EVERY month by waiting.*
