# THE 15-MINUTE STRATEGY CALL: "TITAN AUTHORITY" SCRIPT
## Objective: Transition from "Audit" to "Authority Website System" Close

---

### 1. THE OPENER (2 Minutes)
"Hi [Name], thanks for jumping on. My goal today is simple: Review the Authority Audit we sent over, clarify where your biggest conversion leaks are, and see if our 14-day system is the right fit for where you're taking your business. Sound good?"

### 2. THE DIAGNOSIS (4 Minutes)
"I noticed on your current site that [Problem identified in Audit - e.g., the primary CTA is buried]. When a high-ticket client lands there, what do you think is the first impression they get of your expert status?"

*Listen carefully for pain points like: 'It looks cheap', 'I'm embarrassed', 'It's slow'.*

### 3. THE POSITIONING (4 Minutes)
"Most designers focus on making things 'pretty.' We focus on building **Authority Platforms**.

For someone with your level of expertise, you need a system that:
1. Vetts the client before the call (Our multi-step Planner).
2. Loads in under 1.5 seconds (Our performance architecture).
3. Positions you as the 'Definitive Choice' instantly (Our cinematic branding).

Our **Authority Website System™** does exactly this, and we launch it in 14 days. Does a high-speed, high-performance rebrand like that align with your current goals?"

### 4. THE 14-DAY VELOCITY REVEAL (2 Minutes)
"The reason we can do this in 14 days isn't because we rush — it's because we use a proven 'Authority Architecture' that we've refined over 20 years. We handle the strategy, the copywriting, and the tech. You just show up for the initial blueprint call."

### 5. THE CLOSE / NEXT STEPS (3 Minutes)
"Based on what you've told me, I'm confident we can increase your lead confidence by [Specific Goal].

Our flagship system is a flat investment of [Price - e.g., $5,500]. We have a start date available on [Date].

Shall I send over the proposal and the Strategic Blueprint doc today?"

---

### TACTICAL TIPS FOR SUCCESS:
- **Control the Frame**: You are the specialist, they are the patient.
- **Speed as Value**: Emphasize that 'done is better than perfect' especially when it's built on a proven system.
- **Reference the 'Planner'**: If they filled out the Project Planner, refer back to their budget and goals constantly.
