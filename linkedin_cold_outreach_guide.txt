# TITAN LINKEDIN COLD OUTREACH GUIDE
## High-Ticket Scripts & Strategic Sequences for Agency Owners.

---

### 1. THE "PROFILE FIRST" STRATEGY
Before reaching out, your LinkedIn profile must mirror the Titan Signature theme:
- **Headline**: Expert at closing the Authority Gap for [Niche].
- **Banner**: Cinematic agency branding with a "14-Day Guarantee" callout.
- **Featured**: Link to your ROI Calculator and a Case Study.

---

### 2. SCRIPT A: THE "AUTHORITY SCAN" (Soft Hook)
**Goal**: Get them to agree to a Free Audit.

"Hi [Name], loved your recent post on [Topic]. I noticed your agency is doing great work in [Niche], but your current website might be creating a bit of an 'Authority Gap' compared to your results.

I’m doing 3 manual Authority Scans this week for founders in your space. No sales pitch—just 3 actionable steps to increase your lead quality and speed. Would you like me to run one for [Company Name]?"

---

### 3. SCRIPT B: THE "DIRECT SPRINT" (Aggressive Hook)
**Goal**: Book a strategy call for the 14-day system.

"Hey [Name], I’ve been following [Company Name] and noticed you’re scaling fast. We specialize in building high-performance WordPress systems that command immediate trust.

We’ve just opened up 2 slots for our **14-Day Authority Sprint.** We guarantee a world-class platform in two weeks or we work for free. Are you open to a 10-minute sync to see how we’d close your authority gap?"

---

### 4. THE 3-STEP FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE
- **Day 1**: Initial Outreach (Script A or B).
- **Day 4**: "Just bumping this, [Name]. I saw [Competitor] just updated their site and wanted to make sure you stay market-dominant. Still open to that scan?"
- **Day 9 (The Break-up)**: "I’ll assume a digital rebrand isn't a priority right now, [Name]. I'll close your file for now. If you're ever ready to weaponize your expertise, you know where to find me."

---
*Pro Tip: Target 'Activity' over 'Title'. Reach out to people who are actively posting/hiring.*
