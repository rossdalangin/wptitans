TITAN AGENCY: ELITE TECH STACK GUIDE
===================================

PURPOSE: To ensure maximum operational velocity, security, and performance.

1. INFRASTRUCTURE & HOSTING (The Core)
- Kinsta or WP Engine: Enterprise-grade managed WordPress hosting.
- Cloudflare: Global CDN and WAF (Security Hardening).
- SendGrid or Postmark: For reliable transactional email delivery (Protocols).

2. DESIGN & CREATIVE (The Cinematic UI)
- Figma: For collaborative wireframing and high-fidelity design.
- Adobe Creative Cloud: For luxury asset generation (Noise overlays, textures).
- Loom: For manual Authority Audits and Project Handovers.

3. CONVERSION & LEAD GEN (The Engine)
- Contact Form 7: Theme-integrated and stylized for authority.
- Calendly: Embedded via /book-a-strategy-call/ for high-intent leads.
- Zapier / Make: To automate form-to-email and form-to-CRM workflows.

4. PROJECT MANAGEMENT (The Command Center)
- Slack: For real-time project synchronization.
- ClickUp or Notion: Internal task tracking and Wiki/SOP storage.
- WP Titans Client Portal: The "Front-end" interface for client interaction.

5. SALES & CRM (The Pipeline)
- Pipedrive or HubSpot: For managing Titan-tier opportunities.
- Stripe: For high-ticket deposits and recurring "Titan Maintenance" fees.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TECH STACK RULE:
"Complexity is the enemy of velocity. If a tool doesn't automate a protocol or increase performance, delete it."
