TITAN AGENCY: DISCOVERY CALL BLUEPRINT
--------------------------------------
GOAL: Identify pain, establish authority, and determine if they are a high-value fit.

1. THE OPENER (2 mins)
- "Thanks for hopping on. The goal today is to see where you’re at, where you want to go, and if we're the right team to get you there. Sound good?"

2. THE "WHY NOW?" (5 mins)
- "What made you decide to look for a partner right now?"
- "What happens if you don't solve this problem in the next 6 months?" (Agitation)

3. BUSINESS METRICS (10 mins)
- "What is your current monthly revenue?"
- "What is the average lifetime value of one customer for you?"
- "How many new leads are you currently generating per month?"

4. THE GAP ANALYSIS (10 mins)
- "If we could snap our fingers and fix one thing in your digital presence today, what would it be?"
- "What have you tried in the past that didn't work?"

5. THE AUTHORITY SHIFT (5 mins)
- "Based on what you’ve said, you’re losing about [Calculated Amount] per month in missed opportunity. We usually solve this by [Specific Strategy]."
- "Does it make sense for us to walk through how our 14-Day Sprint works?"

6. THE BUDGET BRIDGING (3 mins)
- "Our growth engagements typically start at [Price]. Is that within the realm of what you had allocated to fix this?"

7. THE NEXT STEPS (5 mins)
- "I'm going to put together a tailored Roadmap based on these numbers."
- "Let's schedule our Strategy Session for [Date/Time]."
