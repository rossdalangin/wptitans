TITAN AGENCY: BRAND VOICE & POSITIONING GUIDE
============================================

TONE: Authoritative, Decisive, Cinematic, High-IQ, and Strategic.

1. THE "TITAN" VOCABULARY
Instead of "Services" --> Use "The Arsenal"
Instead of "Portfolio" --> Use "Success Stories" or "The Gallery of Dominance"
Instead of "Blog" --> Use "Insights" or "The Vault"
Instead of "Standard Build" --> Use "The 14-Day Sprint"
Instead of "Website" --> Use "Digital Authority Platform" or "Revenue Engine"

2. COMMANDING THE CONVERSATION
Titans do not use "passive" language.
- Weak: "We can help you get more leads."
- Titan: "We weaponize your expertise to capture high-intent leads."
- Weak: "Let us know if you're interested."
- Titan: "Initiate Strategy."

3. THE "AUTHORITY GAP" NARRATIVE
Every piece of content must reinforce the Gap.
"You are a master of your craft. But your website says you are a commodity. This Authority Gap is the silent killer of your growth. We are here to close it."

4. VISUAL DIRECTIVES
- Contrast: Deep blacks with high-intensity primary accents (Gold, Sapphire, Emerald).
- Space: Large typography with significant breathing room.
- Motion: Cinematic reveals and physics-based interactions.

5. THE IDEAL CLIENT FILTER
Always speak to the "High-Intent Specialist." Do not cater to generalists or budget-hunters. We are the choice for those who value speed and outcome over "lowest price."
