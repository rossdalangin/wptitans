# REFERRAL & PARTNERSHIP STRATEGY: THE TITAN NETWORK
## Objective: Scale Agency Revenue through Strategic Alliances

---

### 1. THE "COMPLEMENTARY" PARTNER PROFILING
Identify 10 partners in these three categories who serve your "High-Ticket Transitioner" persona but don't build websites:
- **Category A: Brand Photographers** (They see the client right before they need a site).
- **Category B: Business Coaches** (They identify the authority gap first).
- **Category C: Ads Managers** (They can't run traffic to a broken website).

---

### 2. THE TITAN REFERRAL MODEL
Avoid simple "affiliate" links. Use a high-end "Professional Referral" approach:

**The Incentive Structure:**
- **Referrer Reward**: 10% of the project fee ($500 - $1,000) OR a charitable donation in their name.
- **Client Reward**: A "Preferred Partner" discount ($250 off) OR a free "Conversion Audit" add-on.
- **Timing**: Referral fees paid immediately upon receipt of the client's 50% deposit.

---

### 3. PARTNERSHIP OUTREACH SCRIPT (LinkedIn/Email)
"Hi [Partner Name],

I’ve been following your work with [Niche] clients and love your approach to [Specific Skill - e.g., high-end photography].

I run WordPress Titans, and we specialize in 14-day 'Authority Rebrands' for the same experts you work with. Often, our clients come to us needing [Partner's Service] before we can launch their flagship site.

I’d love to see if we can set up a formal referral partnership where I send my clients to you, and vice versa.

Would you be open to a 10-minute chat to align our workflows?

Best,
Ross"

---

### 4. THE "WHITE-GLOVE" HANDOFF PROCESS
To maintain authority, the referral must feel elite:
1. **Intro**: Partner sends a 3-way email intro ("I've found the Titans you need...").
2. **Priority**: Referrals are tagged in the "Project Planner" (add a 'How did you hear about us?' field).
3. **Tracking**: Use a simple shared Google Sheet to track project status for the partner.

---

### 5. STRATEGIC CO-MARKETING
- **The "Expert Panel" Webinar**: Host a live session with a coach and a photographer on "Positioning for 2025."
- **Guest Insights**: Feature a partner in your "Agency Insights" blog and have them share it with their email list.
- **Joint Case Studies**: "How [Client] used [Partner's Service] + The Titan System to double their revenue."
