# STRATEGIC REFERRAL AGREEMENT
## [Agency Name] // Titan Alliance Partner

---

### 1. PURPOSE
This agreement outlines the terms of the referral partnership between [Agency Name] and [Partner Name].

### 2. ELIGIBLE REFERRALS
A "Qualified Referral" is a new client lead who:
- Has not previously engaged with the Agency.
- Signs a contract for a project value of $3,500 or greater.
- Makes the initial deposit payment.

### 3. REFERRAL FEES
- **One-Time Projects**: Partner shall receive a flat fee of $500 per qualified referral.
- **Retainer Services**: Partner shall receive a 10% commission on the first 3 months of any signed "Growth Partner" or "Titan Care" retainer.

### 4. PAYMENT TIMING
Referral fees are paid via [Payment Method] within 15 days of the Agency receiving the client's final project payment (for one-time) or the 3rd month's retainer payment.

### 5. TERM & TERMINATION
This agreement is valid until terminated by either party with 7 days' notice. referrals made prior to termination will still be honored.
