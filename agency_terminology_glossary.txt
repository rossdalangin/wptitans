# THE TITAN GLOSSARY: AGENCY TERMINOLOGY
## Use these terms in your copy and calls to build authority.

---

### 1. THE AUTHORITY GAP
The difference between your real-world expertise and how you appear online. A large gap causes prospects to ghost you despite your results.

### 2. CINEMATIC BRANDING
A high-contrast, luxury design style (like Titan Signature) that uses motion, depth, and typography to create an emotional response of trust and power.

### 3. CONVERSION FRICTION
Any element on a website (long forms, slow speed, confusing navigation) that prevents a user from taking the desired action.

### 4. THE 14-DAY SPRINT
Our proprietary rapid-delivery methodology that deploys a world-class platform in two weeks without sacrificing quality.

### 5. LEAD SCORING
The technical logic used in our Project Planner to automatically identify and prioritize high-budget, high-intent leads for immediate sales follow-up.

### 6. POSITIONING BLUEPRINT
The strategic document created in Phase 1 that defines your unique value proposition, target audience, and core messaging hooks.

### 7. GLASSMORPHISM
A modern UI trend using frosted glass effects and background blurs (Enabled in the Titan Customizer) to create a premium, futuristic feel.

### 8. TITAN CARE
Our comprehensive post-launch support and growth plan that ensures your site stays fast, secure, and authoritative.
