# AGENCY OPERATIONS MANUAL: THE TITAN SYSTEM
## Standard Operating Procedures for Elite 14-Day Delivery.

---

### STAGE 1: ONBOARDING & STRATEGIC BLUEPRINT (Day 1-2)
**Goal**: Absolute clarity on the client's authority and sitemap.

1. **Automation Trigger**: Payment triggers the "Welcome to the Lab" email via the CRM.
2. **Asset Lockdown**: Client is given 24 hours to upload logos, headshots, and case study data to the **Command Center**.
   * *Pro Tip*: No work begins until 100% of critical assets are received. The 14-day clock starts ONLY on receipt.
3. **The Blueprint Call**: Conduct a 45-minute deep-dive using the **Project Kickoff Meeting Agenda**.
   * *Output*: Finalized **Project Discovery Brief** signed by the client.

---

### STAGE 2: AUTHORITY COPY & ARCHITECTURE (Day 3-7)
**Goal**: Crafting the messaging that closes the Authority Gap.

1. **Copy Drafting**: Use the **Website Copywriting Template**. Focus on "Commander" persona.
   * *Layman's terms*: Write like an expert talking to another expert. No fluff.
2. **Architecture**: Create all 17 core pages using the **Titan One-Click Setup**.
3. **Internal Review**: PM reviews the sitemap and initial copy for alignment with the Blueprint.
4. **Client Reveal**: Record a 10-minute Loom video walking through the Copy and Layout Blueprint.

---

### STAGE 3: CINEMATIC BUILD & LOGIC (Day 8-12)
**Goal**: Turning strategy into a high-performance system.

1. **Visual Pass**: Set global colors, typography, and interactive motion (Magnetic buttons/Glassmorphism).
2. **The Engines**: Wire up the ROI Calculator, Project Planner, and Audit forms.
   * *Check*: Test lead scoring logic to ensure "Titan" leads are correctly identified.
3. **Performance Tuning**: Run Lighthouse. Ensure sub-2s load time. Optimize images using Titan custom sizes.
4. **SEO Pass**: Port meta descriptions and set up 301 redirects using the **SEO Handover Checklist**.

---

### STAGE 4: QA, LAUNCH & LEGACY (Day 13-14)
**Goal**: Zero-risk deployment and client empowerment.

1. **The Handshake Call**: 30-minute walkthrough of the backend (Customizer) and Command Center.
2. **Launch Protocol**: Switch DNS, verify SSL, and submit XML sitemap to Google Search Console.
3. **Offboarding**: Send the **Client Handover Guide** and pitch the **Titan Care** maintenance plan.
4. **Social Announcement**: Capture a screenshot for the "Inside the Lab" feed.

---
### THE TITAN VELOCITY RULES:
- **Rule 1**: We do not accept piecemeal feedback. Feedback must be consolidated.
- **Rule 2**: We design for results, not personal preferences. We are the Commanders.
- **Rule 3**: Speed is our brand. Every delay must be documented in the Command Center.
