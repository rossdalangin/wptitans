# CLIENT ONBOARDING QUESTIONNAIRE: TITAN AUTHORITY SYSTEM
## Objective: Facilitate 14-Day Velocity & Absolute Clarity

---

### SECTION 1: BUSINESS IDENTITY & POSITIONING
1. **The 'Why'**: In one sentence, why do you do what you do beyond making money?
2. **The Ideal Client**: Describe the person you want to work with most. What keeps them up at night?
3. **The Core Offer**: What is the primary product or service you want this website to sell?
4. **The Expert Status**: What unique experience or result qualifies you as an 'Authority' in your field?

### SECTION 2: CONVERSION & GOALS
5. **The Primary Action**: When a user lands on your home page, what is the SINGLE action you want them to take? (e.g., Book a Call, Download Guide, Buy Now).
6. **The Current 'Friction'**: What is the biggest objection you hear from prospects before they hire you?
7. **Lead Magnets**: Do you have an existing guide, checklist, or workshop we should feature?

### SECTION 3: VISUAL BRANDING & AESTHETIC
8. **Brand Voice**: If your brand was a car, what would it be and why? (e.g., Tesla = Innovative/Minimalist, Rolls Royce = Traditional Luxury).
9. **Primary Competitors**: List 2-3 websites you admire (or compete with). What do you like/dislike about them?
10. **Key Assets**: Do you have professional photography, brand guidelines, and logos ready for deployment?

### SECTION 4: TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
11. **Existing Infrastructure**: List your current domain provider, hosting, and any CRM you use (HubSpot, Mailchimp, etc.).
12. **Third-Party Integrations**: Do you need specific tools linked? (Calendly, SavvyCal, Stripe, MemberPress).

### SECTION 5: THE 14-DAY COMMITMENT
13. **Final Assets**: To hit our 14-day delivery guarantee, we require all high-resolution images and final content copies by [Date]. Are you able to meet this deadline?
