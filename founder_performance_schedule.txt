# THE TITAN FOUNDER: PEAK PERFORMANCE SCHEDULE
## Optimizing High-IQ Business Management & Scaling.

---

### 06:00 - 08:30 | THE CLARITY BLOCK (Deep Work)
- **Zero Notifications**: No email, Slack, or Social Media.
- **The $1M Task**: Work on the highest-leverage task for the agency (e.g., Refining the Authority System, High-level sales copy, or Strategic partnerships).
- **Physical Optimization**: Hydration + 15 mins of focused movement.

### 09:00 - 11:30 | THE SPRINT COMMAND (Operations)
- **Team Sync**: Quick 15-min standup with PM and Lead Dev to verify 14-day progress.
- **Critical Reviews**: Review live drafts or strategy blueprints before they reach the client.
- **System Hardening**: Identifying bottlenecks in the current SOPs and automating them.

### 12:00 - 14:00 | THE AUTHORITY ENGINE (Sales & Outreach)
- **High-Ticket Sales Calls**: Discovery calls and Design Presentations.
- **Outreach Oversight**: Monitoring LinkedIn cold sequences and Audit delivery.
- **The "Titan Alliance"**: Networking with potential strategic partners.

### 14:30 - 16:30 | THE LEGACY BLOCK (Marketing & Learning)
- **Thought Leadership**: Drafting LinkedIn content or scripts for the next VSL/Video Ad.
- **High-IQ Learning**: Studying latest trends in AI, UX psychology, or agency scaling.
- **Financial Review**: Weekly check of the Marketing ROI Tracker and Pipeline.

### 17:00 - 20:00 | THE DECOMPRESSION (Reset)
- **Full Offline**: Disconnect from all agency systems.
- **Family & Health**: High-quality nutrition and focused reset.
- **Sleep Optimization**: Preparing for the next 06:00 clarity block.
