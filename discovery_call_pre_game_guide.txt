TITAN AGENCY: DISCOVERY CALL PRE-GAME GUIDE
========================================

PURPOSE: To ensure the agency founder enters every discovery call with maximum leverage and authority.

1. THE 15-MINUTE RECONNAISSANCE
Before the call, find the answers to these four questions:
- What is their "Authority Gap"? (Do they look like an expert or a commodity?)
- Who are their top 3 competitors? (Do the competitors look more "premium"?)
- What is their primary "Conversion Leak"? (Broken forms, poor CTAs, slow load times?)
- What is their estimated revenue? (Check LinkedIn company size/industry averages).

2. THE "AUTHORITY" ENVIRONMENT
- Background: Ensure a professional, clutter-free environment.
- Audio: Use a high-quality external microphone. Elite agencies don't have "echo."
- Lighting: Face a window or use a ring light. Shadowy founders look untrustworthy.

3. THE OPENING FRAME (The first 2 minutes)
Do NOT start with "How are you?"
Start with: "I've spent the last 20 minutes reviewing your current platform and your competitors in the [Industry] space. I have some very specific thoughts on the Gap we need to close. But first, tell me—what is the singular business outcome you need this website to deliver in the next 90 days?"

4. KEY DISCOVERY QUESTIONS
- "If we did nothing and stayed with the current site for another year, what does that cost the business in lost revenue?" (The Cost of Inaction).
- "On a scale of 1 to 10, how well does your current site reflect the actual quality of your work?" (The Authority Score).
- "We deliver our flagship builds in 14 days. Does your team have the internal capacity to handle that velocity right now?" (The Urgency Test).

5. THE CLOSING SEQUENCE
- Do not ask: "What do you think?"
- Say: "Based on what we discussed, you are a perfect fit for our Authority System™. Here is how we initialize the 14-Day Sprint. I'll send over the Roadmap and the MSA now. Do you have any technical questions before we finalize the slot for [Date]?"
