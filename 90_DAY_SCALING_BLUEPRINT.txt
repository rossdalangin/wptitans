TITAN AGENCY: 90-DAY SCALING BLUEPRINT
========================================

PURPOSE: To move from a solo-expert to a high-output agency architecture without losing creative authority.

- Week 1: Deploy the Titan Ecosystem. Map all forms and assets using the FINAL_SETUP_GUIDE.txt.
- Week 2: Content Dominance. Record 4 authority-based Loom videos (Audits) for prospective high-ticket leads.
- Week 3: Price Hardening. Set your "Authority Website System" price at a minimum of $4,500.
- Week 4: First Win. Secure one 14-Day Sprint client. Document every step for your first Case Study.

MONTH 2: OPERATIONAL VELOCITY

MONTH 3: THE SCALE TO $1M+ ARR

- Week 10: Creative Directorship. Shift your role from "Doer" to "Reviewer." Your team builds; you verify authority.
- Week 11: Pricing Evolution. Increase flagship price to $7,500+ based on the documented ROI of Month 1-2 wins.
- Week 12: Authority Exit. Your agency now operates with 80% team autonomy. You focus on high-level strategy calls.
