### 4. PSYCHOLOGICAL PRICING TIPS
- **The Decoy Effect**: Use Tier 3 to make Tier 2 look like an obvious bargain.
- **Flat Fee Confidence**: Avoid hourly rates. High-ticket clients value results and speed, not hours spent.
- **Value-Based Anchoring**: "A single high-ticket client secured through this system pays for the entire investment. Everything after that is pure profit."
