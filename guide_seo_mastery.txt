# SEO MASTERY FOR HIGH-TICKET AGENCIES
## The Roadmap to First-Page Dominance and High-Intent Traffic.

---

### 1. THE AUTHORITY KEYWORD STRATEGY
Don't chase volume. Chase intent. High-ticket clients don't search for "web design." They search for "authority branding for consultants" or "high-performance WordPress for law firms."
- **Focus**: Long-tail, niche-specific keywords.
- **Goal**: 100 high-intent visitors are worth more than 10,000 "cheap" visitors.

### 2. TECHNICAL PERFORMANCE (CORE WEB VITALS)
Google now prioritizes user experience above all else. Your Titan site is built for speed, but you must maintain it.
- **Sub-2s Loads**: Anything slower kills your ranking.
- **Mobile First**: 60%+ of high-ticket discovery happens on mobile.
- **Clean Code**: Minimize plugin bloat (The Titan way).

### 3. THE BACKLINK ALLIANCE
Earn links from other authoritative sources in your niche.
- **Guest Posting**: Write for industry publications.
- **Case Studies**: Share your results-driven data.
- **Partnerships**: Link exchanges with non-competing strategic partners.

### 4. ON-PAGE OPTIMIZATION CHECKLIST
[ ] Singular keyword-rich H1.
[ ] Descriptive Meta Titles & Descriptions (See Titan SEO settings).
[ ] High-resolution images with optimized Alt tags.
[ ] Internal linking structure following the Strategic Sitemap.
