# STRATEGIC SITEMAP & ARCHITECTURE TEMPLATE
## Closing the Authority Gap through Structural Clarity.

---

### LEVEL 1: CORE AUTHORITY HUB
1. **Homepage (The Commander)**: Core positioning, lead magnet (Audit), and trust bars.
2. **Flagship Landing Page (The System)**: Long-form sales page for the primary high-ticket service.
3. **About (The Legacy)**: Founder's story, values, and team authority.

### LEVEL 2: CONVERSION ENGINES
1. **Services Archive (The Arsenal)**: Categorized overview of all agency solutions.
   - *Sub-page*: Service Detail (Specific workflows and case studies).
2. **Case Studies (The Proof)**: High-impact portfolio items with ROI focus.
3. **Project Planner (The Filter)**: Multi-step AJAX qualification engine.

### LEVEL 3: LEAD MAGNETS & TOOLS
1. **Free Authority Audit (The Hook)**: Specialized capture page for manual reviews.
2. **ROI Calculator (The Logic)**: Demonstrating the financial value of a rebuild.
3. **Client Portal (The HQ)**: Secure Command Center for active project management.
4. **Resources/Vault (The Value)**: Free guides, blueprints, and educational content.

### LEVEL 4: TRUST & LEGAL
1. **Contact (The Handshake)**: Low-friction direct communication.
2. **Privacy Policy**: Standard data protection.
3. **Terms & Conditions**: Operational legalities.

---

### THE "GOLDEN PATH" NAVIGATION (UX Strategy)
- **Primary Path**: Home -> Flagship Page -> Project Planner -> Strategy Call.
- **Secondary Path**: Home -> Case Studies -> Free Audit -> Strategy Call.
- **Navigation Priority**: Keep the top-right CTA consistent (e.g., "Book a Call") across all levels.
