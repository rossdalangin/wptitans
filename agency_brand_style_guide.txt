# TITAN BRAND STYLE GUIDE: DESIGN STANDARDS
## Maintaining the Luxury Aesthetic of the WordPress Titans Ecosystem

---

### 1. TYPOGRAPHY (The Syne/Space Grotesk Duo)
- **Headings (Syne)**:
  - Weight: 800 (Extra Bold) for H1, 700 for others.
  - Case: Uppercase for primary taglines and section headers.
  - Letter Spacing: -0.04em for H1 (Tight), +0.2em for Taglines (Wide).
- **Body (Space Grotesk)**:
  - Weight: 300 (Light) for long-form, 500 (Medium) for UI labels.
  - Line Height: 1.8 (Elite readability).

---

### 2. COLOR PALETTE
- **Midnight Black (#000000)**: The base of power. Use for all main backgrounds.
- **Titan Gold (#D4AF37)**: The color of authority. Use for primary CTAs, icons, and accents.
- **Titan Secondary Gold (#B8860B)**: Deep gold for gradients and hover states.
- **Glass/Border (#FFFFFF, Opacity 0.08)**: Use for subtle section separators and card borders.
- **Text Dim (#CCCCCC)**: Primary body color to reduce eye strain on dark backgrounds.

---

### 3. VISUAL LANGUAGE (The Titan "Vibe")
- **Gradients**: Use radial gradients (Gold to Transparent) for "Mouse Glow" effects.
- **Borders**: All primary cards and buttons must have a 8px (Customizer-managed) border-radius.
- **Shadows**: Avoid heavy black shadows. Use "Glow" shadows (Titan Gold at 15% opacity) for high-intent elements.
- **Imagery**: Use high-contrast, professional photography. Prefer desaturated or "Darkened" images (80% overlay) in heroes to ensure headline legibility.

---

### 4. INTERACTION STANDARDS
- **Magnetic Force**: All primary buttons must attract the cursor within a 50px radius.
- **Glassmorphism**: When using "Glass Luxury" mode, ensure the background blur is set to at least 15px.
- **Transitions**: All hover states must use the `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` timing function for a "snappy yet smooth" luxury feel.
