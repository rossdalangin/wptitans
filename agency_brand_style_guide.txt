# TITAN BRAND STYLE GUIDE: DESIGN STANDARDS
## Maintaining the "Voice of Power" across Visual and Verbal Assets.

---

### 1. VISUAL DO'S AND DON'TS

**DO:**
- Use high-contrast backgrounds (Deep Black #000).
- Use Gold (#D4AF37) for "Intentional" highlights only (CTAs, Key Icons).
- Maintain generous whitespace (Padding: 10rem+).
- Use subtle textures (Titan patterns) to add "High-Grade" depth.

**DON'T:**
- Use low-contrast gray text on black (Hard to read, feels cheap).
- Use generic "Corporate" blue or bright primary colors.
- Use cluttered layouts or overlapping text on mobile.
- Use low-resolution or generic stock photos.

---

### 2. TYPOGRAPHY HIERARCHY
- **H1 (Headlines)**: Syne (Weight 800). Letter spacing: -0.04em.
- **H2/H3 (Sub-headers)**: Syne (Weight 700). Uppercase enabled.
- **Body**: Space Grotesk (Weight 300). Line height: 1.8.
- **Labels**: Space Grotesk (Weight 700). Uppercase, +0.2em letter spacing.

---

### 3. THE "INTERACTIVE LUXURY" PRINCIPLE
Every user interaction should feel "bespoke."
- **Magnetic Force**: Buttons should attract the cursor within a 50px radius.
- **Glassmorphism**: Use for overlays to simulate a "Modern Studio" feel.
- **Reveal Motion**: Content should "reveal" vertically or swipe horizontally to maintain momentum.

---

### 4. BRAND VOICE: THE COMMANDER PERSONA
- **Layman's term**: We are the doctor, the client is the patient. We diagnose, we don't ask for permission.
- **Hook**: "Close the Authority Gap."
- **Guarantee**: "Velocity without Compromise."
