TITAN MASTER AGENCY SOP: THE OPERATIONAL BIBLE
=============================================

This document outlines the standard operating procedures (SOPs) for running a high-velocity, high-ticket WordPress agency using the Titan Ecosystem.

1. LEAD GENERATION (The Daily Engine)
------------------------------------
- Morning (8:00 AM): Review the "Project Planner" submissions.
  - Score leads: Budget > $5k = Priority 1.
  - Manual follow-up: Send a personalized video (Loom) addressing their specific "Authority Gap."
- Afternoon (2:00 PM): Deliver "Free Authority Audits."
  - Follow the 30-point `conversion_audit_checklist.txt`.
  - Goal: Book a Gap Discovery Call.
- Continuous: Monitor LinkedIn/Twitter. Post one cinematic project insight every 48 hours.

2. SALES & POSITIONING (The Conversion Protocol)
-----------------------------------------------
- Step 1: Pre-Call Reconnaissance. Use `discovery_call_pre_game_guide.txt`.
- Step 2: The Discovery Call. Focus on the "Cost of Inaction."
- Step 3: Immediate Follow-up. Send the post-booking automation sequence Day 3 (Bridge Email).
- Step 4: Proposal Delivery. Use the `strategic_sitemap_template.txt` to visualize the outcome.

3. THE 14-DAY VELOCITY SPRINT (Project Delivery)
------------------------------------------------
- DAY 0: Onboarding. Client completes the Onboarding Protocol page.
- DAY 1-3: Strategy & Copywriting.
  - Deliver Brand Messaging Blueprint.
  - Client approval via Client Portal message board.
- DAY 4-7: Cinematic Design.
  - Build out the 5-page Authority Framework.
  - Use the "Lava Lamp" and "Glassmorphism" presets for high-end feel.
- DAY 8-12: Technical Development & SEO.
  - Harden security. Optimize for < 2s load time.
  - Implement dynamic JSON-LD Schema.
- DAY 13: Final Quality Assurance.
  - Perform "Performance Audit" using `website_performance_audit_report.txt`.
- DAY 14: THE LAUNCH.
  - Switch to Live. Deliver handover pack via Client Portal.

4. CLIENT MANAGEMENT (The Command Center)
-----------------------------------------
- Rule 1: All assets go to the Client Portal. Never use email for file transfers.
- Rule 2: Weekly Velocity Updates. Every Friday, post a project status update in the Portal.
- Rule 3: Testimonial Capture. On Day 30 post-launch, trigger the `client_testimonial_request_template.txt`.

5. AGENCY SCALING (The Growth Roadmap)
--------------------------------------
- Quarterly: Review `agency_growth_metrics_dashboard.csv`.
- White-Labeling: When project load exceeds 3 per month, initialize the `guide_white_label_partnerships.txt` to outsource development while maintaining Creative Directorship.
- Pricing: Review and increase flagship price by 10% every 5 successful launches.
