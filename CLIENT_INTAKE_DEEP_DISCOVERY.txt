TITAN AGENCY: CLIENT INTAKE & DEEP DISCOVERY PROTOCOL
===================================================

PURPOSE: To extract the master-level expertise from the client to feed the Authority Engine.

SECTION 1: THE BUSINESS NUCLEUS
1. What is the singular, most expensive problem you solve for your clients?
2. If you had to describe your unique methodology in 3 words, what are they?
3. Who is your absolute "Dream Client" (Name a specific company or person)?
4. What is the #1 objection you hear on sales calls?
5. What is the lifetime value (LTV) of a single high-ticket client?

SECTION 2: THE AUTHORITY GAP
6. Why should a prospect choose you over a competitor who is 50% cheaper?
7. What is the biggest myth or lie in your industry that you want to debunk?
8. What is the "Secret Sauce" in your process that no one else is doing?
9. List 3 brand names (any industry) that you admire for their positioning.
10. On a scale of 1-10, how much does your current website embarrass you?

SECTION 3: CONVERSION & ASSETS
11. What is the primary action you want a visitor to take (e.g., Book Call, Download Audit)?
12. Do you have high-resolution headshots and project images ready for Day 4?
13. List 3 recent client success stories with quantified results (%, $, or Time).
14. What are the top 5 questions your prospects ask during discovery?
15. Do you have a lead magnet (PDF/Video) that we need to integrate?

SECTION 4: TECHNICAL & LOGISTICS
16. Current domain registrar and hosting provider?
17. Do you have access to your Google Analytics / Search Console?
18. Which CRM are you using to manage leads?
19. List any specific 3rd-party integrations required (LMS, Booking, API).
20. Who is the internal "Mission Commander" for this project (Primary contact)?

SECTION 5: THE VISION (Long-Term)
21. Where do you want this brand to be in 24 months?
22. Are you planning to sell the agency/business in the next 5 years?
23. What does "Success" look like for this 14-Day Velocity Sprint?
