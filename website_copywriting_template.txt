# TITAN SIGNATURE: WEBSITE COPYWRITING TEMPLATE
## Closing the Authority Gap via Direct-Response Copywriting.

---

### THE PERSONA: THE "COMMANDER"
Your copy should not "help" or "assist." It should lead. Use active verbs and authoritative framing.

---

### PAGE: HOMEPAGE

**1. HERO SECTION**
- **Headline (The Hook)**: "Weaponize Your Expertise with a World-Class Authority Platform."
- **Sub-headline**: "Stop losing high-ticket deals to a 'starter kit' website. We deploy high-performance systems for experts who demand immediate trust and unstoppable growth."
- **CTA**: "Claim Your Free Audit"

**2. THE PAIN POINT (The Authority Gap)**
- **Headline**: "You Are an Expert. Why Do You Look Like a Beginner Online?"
- **Copy**: "You’ve spent decades mastering your craft. You deliver 7-figure results. But your current website creates a 'friction point' that causes your best prospects to ghost you. We close that gap in 14 days."

---

### PAGE: SERVICES (The Authority System™)

**1. STRATEGY BLUEPRINT**
- **Draft**: "We don't start with colors; we start with results. Our 45-minute blueprint session nails your positioning so your site closes deals before you even speak."

**2. CINEMATIC DESIGN**
- **Draft**: "Luxury isn't a look; it's a feeling of power. We use high-contrast UI, motion depth, and custom typography to ensure you command the room."

---

### COPYWRITING "BEFORE & AFTER" (Real Examples)

- **BEFORE (Weak)**: "We build websites for consultants and help you get more traffic from Google."
- **AFTER (Titan)**: "We deploy high-performance conversion engines that position consultants as the definitive choice in their market."

- **BEFORE (Weak)**: "Our process takes about 2 weeks and we will show you some designs for feedback."
- **AFTER (Titan)**: "Our systematic 14-day sprint weaponizes your brand and transforms your digital presence with elite precision."

---
*Pro Tip: Every sentence should answer the client's internal question: "How does this make me look like a market leader?"*
