# WEBSITE MAINTENANCE CHECKLIST (WEEKLY/MONTHLY)
## Maintaining Authority through Technical Excellence

---

### WEEKLY TASKS
[ ] **Backups**: Verify that a full site backup was successful and stored off-site.
[ ] **Updates**: Install WordPress core, plugin, and theme updates (Check for conflicts).
[ ] **Security**: Scan for malware or unauthorized login attempts.
[ ] **Forms**: Perform a test submission of the Project Planner and Contact Form.
[ ] **Uptime**: Check uptime monitoring reports for any downtime.

---

### MONTHLY TASKS
[ ] **Performance**: Run a Google PageSpeed Insights test. Aim for 90+ on Desktop.
[ ] **Links**: Scan for broken links (404 errors) and fix or redirect them.
[ ] **Database**: Optimize the WordPress database (Clean up revisions and overhead).
[ ] **SEO**: Review Google Search Console for any indexing errors or search drops.
[ ] **Content**: Update copyright date and any seasonal offers in the Customizer.

---

### QUARTERLY TASKS
[ ] **UX Review**: Navigate your site on a mobile device you don't usually use. Check the "Thumb Test."
[ ] **Legal**: Review Privacy Policy and Terms for any required updates.
[ ] **Password Change**: Update administrative passwords for increased security.
[ ] **Strategic Review**: Does the messaging still align with your current business goals?

---
*A Titan site is a living asset. Keep it sharp.*
