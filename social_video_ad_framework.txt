# SOCIAL MEDIA VIDEO AD FRAMEWORK: THE TITAN HOOK
## Objective: Short-form Video (Reels/TikTok/LinkedIn) for Lead Gen

---

### AD CONCEPT 1: THE "FRUSTRATED EXPERT" (Problem/Solution)
**Visual**: Start with a screen recording of a slow, generic, "free-template" looking website. Use a "Thinking/Confused" emoji overlay.
**Hook (Text/Voiceover)**: "Is your website actually hurting your authority?"
**Body**:
"You’ve spent years becoming an expert. But your website looks like you started yesterday. That 'Authority Gap' is why clients are ghosting you after they visit your site."
**Transition**: Swipe to a cinematic Titan Signature site with the Noise Overlay and Glass mode.
**The Solution**:
"At Titan, we build high-performance platforms that command immediate trust. And we do it in 14 days, guaranteed. Stop settles for a brochure. Build a conversion engine."
**CTA**: Click below for your free Authority Scan.

---

### AD CONCEPT 2: THE "14-DAY CLOCK" (Speed/Social Proof)
**Visual**: A fast-motion montage of the Titan Customizer being used, alternating with a digital countdown timer (14:00:00).
**Hook**: "A world-class authority platform in 14 days. No excuses."
**Body**:
"Tired of agencies that take 3 months to deliver a basic site? Our systematic approach takes the definitive leaders in [Niche] from 'Outdated' to 'Market Dominant' in two weeks."
**Proof**: Quick flash of the "Before & After" slider and a +140% Growth metric card.
**CTA**: Request your audit. Link in bio.

---

### AD CONCEPT 3: THE "INTERACTIVE HOOK" (UX Showcase)
**Visual**: Zoomed-in footage of the "Magnetic Button" effect and the "Custom Cursor VIEW label" in action.
**Hook**: "Your website should feel like a premium experience."
**Body**:
"Luxury branding is in the details. From our magnetic conversion buttons to our reactive custom cursors, we build sites that people *want* to stay on. Because time on site = trust built."
**CTA**: See the system in action.

---

### PRODUCTION DIRECTIVES:
- **Audio**: Use deep, rhythmic "Luxury/Techno" or "Cinematic Orchestral" tracks.
- **Lighting**: High-contrast, low-key lighting if filming the founder.
- **Editing**: Fast cuts (every 1.5 - 2 seconds) to maintain attention.
- **Text Overlays**: Use the theme's 'Syne' font (or similar bold sans-serif) for all video captions to maintain brand consistency.
