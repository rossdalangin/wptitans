# AGENCY GROWTH ROADMAP: FROM $0 TO $1M ARR
## The Titan Scaling Path

---

### STAGE 1: THE AUTHORITY FOUNDATION ($0 - $10k/mo)
**Focus**: Niche selection, Flagship offer (The 14-Day Sprint), and Cold Outreach.
- [ ] Define a high-value niche (e.g., SaaS Founders, Law Firms, Medical Specialists).
- [ ] Launch your own Titan Authority Site.
- [ ] Implement the "100 Reachouts/Week" Cold LinkedIn strategy.
- [ ] Close 2-3 projects at $3.5k - $5k each.
- [ ] Document every step for your first case study.

---

### STAGE 2: THE RECURRING ENGINE ($10k - $30k/mo)
**Focus**: Retention, Paid Ads, and First Hire.
- [ ] Upsell 80% of clients into the "Titan Care" maintenance plan.
- [ ] Launch the "Growth Partner" retainer ($997/mo+) for performance optimization.
- [ ] Hire a Junior WordPress Specialist to handle Stage 3 of the operations manual.
- [ ] Start Meta/LinkedIn Ads targeting the "Free Authority Audit" lead magnet.
- [ ] Achieve a 50% referral rate through the Referral Strategy.

---

### STAGE 3: THE SCALE SYSTEM ($30k - $80k/mo)
**Focus**: Sales Team, Content Dominance, and Enterprise Projects.
- [ ] Hire a dedicated Appointment Setter (SDR).
- [ ] Scale ad spend to $3k - $5k/mo.
- [ ] Transition from $5k projects to $15k+ Enterprise solutions.
- [ ] Dominate your niche's LinkedIn feed with daily thought leadership.
- [ ] Build a library of 10+ high-impact case studies showing 200%+ ROI.

---

### STAGE 4: THE MARKET TITAN ($1M+ ARR)
**Focus**: Brand legacy, Workshops, and Full Operations Team.
- [ ] Hire a full-time Creative Director and Project Manager.
- [ ] Launch a "Titan Academy" or Authority Workshop for your niche.
- [ ] Founder moves into a purely strategic/visionary role.
- [ ] Industry recognition through awards and media appearances.
- [ ] Expansion into complementary services (e.g., CRM architecture, full-funnel management).

---
*Consistency is the only secret.*
