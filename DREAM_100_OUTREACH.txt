TITAN AGENCY: DREAM 100 OUTREACH PROTOCOL
========================================

PURPOSE: To target and secure the top 100 most influential potential clients in your niche.

1. THE LIST BUILDING
- Identify 100 "Dream" clients who have the budget and the mastery, but a clear "Authority Gap."
- Metrics for inclusion: High-value offer, outdated digital presence, active on social/content.

2. THE "GIFTING" PHASE (Weeks 1-4)
- Day 1: Follow and engage with their content (High-IQ comments only).
- Day 7: Send a "Reverse Audit" video (5 mins, value-only, no pitch).
  - Script: "I've been following your work on [Topic] and it's world-class. I noticed a friction point on your [Page] that might be costing you leads. I made this video to show you exactly how to fix it."
- Day 14: Send a relevant physical gift or a high-level digital resource from "The Vault."

3. THE "DIRECT ASK" (Week 6)
- The Frame: "We've established the gap. Now let's close it."
- Script: "I've sketched out a 14-Day Velocity Roadmap for [Company]. Based on our initial scan, this could increase your lead quality by $[X]. Do you have 15 minutes for a Gap Discovery call on [Day]?"

4. MULTI-CHANNEL REINFORCEMENT
- LinkedIn: Engagement and Direct Messaging.
- Twitter/X: Public praise of their work.
- Email: ROI-based follow-ups using the `ACQUISITION_BATTLEPLAN.txt` logic.

5. THE TITAN CLOSER
- If they say "We're happy with our current agency,"
- Response: "I understand. Most agencies are great at maintenance. We specialize in Authority Transformation. If you're ever ready to move from 'Functional' to 'Dominant,' here is the link to my private calendar."

"A Titan doesn't beg for business; a Titan presents an opportunity for dominance."
