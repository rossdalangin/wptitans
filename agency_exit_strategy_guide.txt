# AGENCY EXIT STRATEGY & VALUATION GUIDE
## Building a Sellable High-Ticket Agency Asset.

---

### 1. THE VALUATION MULTIPLIERS
Agencies typically sell for 2x to 5x of SDE (Seller's Discretionary Earnings).
- **2x-3x**: Small boutique, heavy owner involvement, low recurring revenue.
- **4x-5x+**: Systems-driven (SOPs), high recurring revenue (Titan Care), clean financial history.

---

### 2. THE 3 PILLARS OF EXIT READINESS
**A. SOP Dominance**: Every part of the 14-day sprint must be documented. If you (the founder) disappear and the agency keeps running, the value doubles.
**B. Revenue Mix**: Shift from 100% one-time projects to at least 40% recurring revenue. Maintenance and Growth Partner retainers are "High-Value" income to buyers.
**C. Lead Generation Independence**: A buyer wants to see that leads come from a system (SEO/Ads/Titan Theme) and not just the founder's personal network.

---

### 3. THE EXIT ROADMAP
- **Phase 1 (18-24 mo out)**: Optimize margins. Use the Pricing & Margin Calculator to ensure 50%+ net profit.
- **Phase 2 (12 mo out)**: Remove yourself from delivery. Hire a Project Manager and Lead Developer to handle all client communication.
- **Phase 3 (6 mo out)**: Audit all legal contracts. Ensure all MSA and NDA documents are signed and archived.
- **Phase 4 (3 mo out)**: Engage an Agency M&A Broker.

---

### 4. KEY METRICS FOR BUYERS
- **LTV** (Lifetime Value of a Client).
- **CAC** (Customer Acquisition Cost).
- **Churn Rate** (For recurring plans).
- **Profit Margin** per 14-Day Sprint.

---
*Build your agency as if you are going to sell it tomorrow, but keep it as if you will run it forever.*
