# THE 14-DAY VELOCITY: THE SPRINT OPERATIONS BIBLE
## The Step-by-Step Framework for Rapid, High-Quality Delivery.

---

### WHY VELOCITY IS YOUR COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE
Clients hate 6-month projects. They lose momentum and interest. By launching in 14 days, you become a "Results Provider," not a "Service Provider."

---

### THE 14-DAY CALENDAR (GRANULAR BREAKDOWN)

**DAY 1-2: DISCOVERY & BLUEPRINT (The Strategy)**
- **Task**: 45-min Kickoff call. Finalize the "Authority Hook" and Sitemap.
- **Client Action**: Must upload all logos, headshots, and existing case study data.
- **Pro Tip**: Use the **Project Discovery Brief** template to get a signed approval before moving to Day 3.

**DAY 3-5: THE BLUEPRINT & COPY (The Message)**
- **Task**: Senior copywriter drafts the Home, About, and top 3 Service pages.
- **Focus**: High-converting hooks and pain-point relief.
- **Status**: Send to client for ONE round of feedback on Day 5.

**DAY 6-8: DESIGN & REVEAL (The Visuals)**
- **Task**: Apply the Titan Design Preset. Build the core pages in WordPress.
- **Focus**: Cinematic branding, magnetic buttons, and glassmorphism settings.
- **Handover**: Live video walkthrough (Loom) of the draft for client approval.

**DAY 9-11: DEVELOPMENT & WIRING (The Logic)**
- **Task**: Configure the ROI Calculator, Project Planner, and Exit-Intent modals.
- **Focus**: 100% functionality and lead-scoring accuracy.

**DAY 12-13: PERFORMANCE & SEO (The Optimization)**
- **Task**: Lighthouse speed testing. Porting SEO metadata. 301 Redirect map.
- **Benchmark**: Sub-2s load time. 90+ Mobile Score.

**DAY 14: LAUNCH & COMMAND CENTER (The Handover)**
- **Task**: DNS Switch. Client Portal training call.
- **The Upsell**: Present the "Titan Care" maintenance plan for long-term security.

---

### THE "GOLDEN RULES" OF THE SPRINT
1. **No Asset, No Clock**: The 14 days start ONLY when the client provides 100% of the required materials.
2. **Consolidated Feedback**: One document. One time. We do not do "endless revisions."
3. **System Over Personal Taste**: We are the experts. We design for conversions, not for the client's favorite color.
