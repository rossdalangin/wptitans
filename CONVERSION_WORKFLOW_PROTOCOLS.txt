TITAN AGENCY: CONVERSION WORKFLOW PROTOCOLS
==========================================

This document provides explicit instructions for agency owners on how to set up the form logic, redirects, and asset delivery pipelines for maximum conversion.

1. THE "STRATEGY CALL" PROTOCOL (Direct Conversion)
--------------------------------------------------
- Form Location: /book-a-strategy-call/
- Tool: Calendly, SavvyCal, or WP Titans Book Template.
- Logic: Client selects a time.
- Next Page: Success/Confirmation page (auto-redirect from Calendly).
- What they get: Calendar invite + Pre-Call Authority Pack (sent via automated email).
- Founder Action: Review client website/social before the call.

2. THE "AUTHORITY AUDIT" PROTOCOL (High-Value Lead Gen)
------------------------------------------------------
- Form Location: /free-authority-audit/
- Logic: Single or multi-step form capturing Website URL, Revenue Tier, and Goals.
- Next Page: /thank-you-audit/ (Features a video from the founder setting expectations).
- What they get: Manual "Authority Scan" (Loom video or PDF) delivered within 24-48 hours.
- Founder Action: Deliver the audit and offer a "Gap Discovery Call" to discuss results.

3. THE "PROJECT PLANNER" PROTOCOL (Qualification)
------------------------------------------------
- Form Location: /project-planner/
- Logic: 5-Step AJAX form in the WP Titans theme.
- High-Value Redirect: If budget is > $5,000, redirect to /book-a-strategy-call/.
- Low-Value Redirect: If budget is < $2,000, redirect to /resource-center/ (Self-serve).
- What they get: A summarized "Project Roadmap" email based on their inputs.
- Founder Action: Prioritize "Titan" leads for immediate manual outreach.

4. THE "EXIT INTENT" PROTOCOL (Recovery)
----------------------------------------
- Form Location: Triggered Modal (Pop-up).
- Logic: Captures Email only.
- Next Page: Success Message + stay on current page.
- What they get: "The $100k Authority Blueprint" (PDF download).
- Founder Action: Set up a 5-part email nurture sequence (Day 1, 3, 7, 14, 21).

5. THE "ROI CALCULATOR" PROTOCOL (Logic Lead)
---------------------------------------------
- Location: /roi-calculator/
- Interaction: JS-based calculator.
- CTA Logic: "Capture This ROI" button.
- Next Page: /free-authority-audit/ (Pre-fills URL if possible).
- What they get: The realization of the revenue they are losing (The Authority Gap).

6. THE "PARTNER INTAKE" PROTOCOL (Scale)
---------------------------------------
- Form Location: /referral-partner-protocol/
- Logic: Questions on industry focus and current client load.
- Next Page: /partner-onboarding/ (Includes the Partner Kit and Agreements).
- What they get: Referral Fee Schedule + Brand Assets + White-label SOPs.

- Recovery CTA --> /resource-center/
