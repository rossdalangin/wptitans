# THE OBJECTION VAULT: HIGH-TICKET REBUTTALS
## Psychological Frameworks for Overcoming "Titan" Hesitations.

---

### 1. THE "PRICE" OBJECTION
**Client**: "This is more than we budgeted for."
**Rebuttal (The ROI Anchor)**: "I completely understand. But let's look at the numbers. Your current site is leaking $[Amount] per month. If we wait 3 months to find a 'cheaper' option, you've lost $[3x Amount]. The 'expensive' option is actually the one that keeps the leak open. Our system pays for itself by closing that gap. Does it make sense to lose $10k to save $2k?"

---

### 2. THE "TIME" OBJECTION
**Client**: "I'm too busy to start a web project right now."
**Rebuttal (The Burden Shift)**: "That is exactly why the 14-Day Sprint exists. Most projects drag on for 6 months because they require too much of your time. Our system is designed to take the burden *off* you. We only need 45 minutes for the initial blueprint. We handle the rest. If we wait until you're 'less busy,' you'll still have a site that isn't working for you. Let's fix it now so the site starts saving you time by qualifying your leads automatically."

---

### 3. THE "TRUST" OBJECTION
**Client**: "How do I know you can deliver in 14 days?"
**Rebuttal (The Risk Reversal)**: "That’s why we have our **Success Guarantee.** If we miss that 14-day mark for your initial draft, we cut our fee in half. We’ve built our entire agency around this velocity. You can also see our [Case Study Vault] where we’ve done this for [Niche] experts just like you."

---

### 4. THE "DIY" OBJECTION
**Client**: "Can't I just use Wix or Squarespace?"
**Rebuttal (The Quality Signal)**: "You could. But high-ticket prospects can 'smell' a DIY site in seconds. It signals 'small' and 'unproven.' A Titan platform uses high-performance architecture, custom motion, and authority-first copywriting that DIY tools simply can't replicate. Do you want your brand to look like a hobby, or like a market leader?"

---
*THE TITAN RULE: Never argue. Always empathize, then re-frame the perspective around Authority and ROI.*
