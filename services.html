Operational FAQ

What makes Vulcan different from a standard IT company?
IT companies focus on functionality. Vulcan focuses on survival. We assume the system is already breached and work backwards to find the intruder.
Is offensive pentesting legal for my business?
Yes. It is performed under a strict legal contract (Rules of Engagement) that authorizes us to test your defenses without disrupting operations.
How long does a full security audit take?
Typically between 2 to 6 weeks, depending on the complexity of your network and the number of end-points.
Do you provide 24/7 incident response?
Our Global Security Operations Center (GSOC) is active 24/7/365, with an emergency response time of less than 15 minutes.
Can you secure legacy industrial hardware?
Yes. We specialize in "legacy hardening," adding layers of modern protection to older systems that cannot be easily replaced.
What is Zero-Trust exactly?
It is a security model that requires strict identity verification for every person and device trying to access resources on a private network.
Do you offer cybersecurity training for staff?
Yes. We offer Phishing simulation and social engineering awareness workshops to ensure your employees aren't the weakest link.
How does AI help in cybersecurity?
Our AI processes billions of data points to recognize patterns of "abnormal behavior" that a human monitor would never see in real-time.
