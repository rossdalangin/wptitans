# AGENCY TECH STACK GUIDE: THE ELITE TOOLKIT
## The Tools Required to Run a High-Ticket, 14-Day Sprint Agency.

---

### 1. PLATFORM & INFRASTRUCTURE
- **CMS**: WordPress (Self-hosted).
- **Theme**: Titan Signature Legacy (The Foundation).
- **Hosting**: WP Engine or Cloudways (For sub-2s load times).
- **Domain/DNS**: Cloudflare (For proxy protection and performance).

### 2. LEAD GEN & CONVERSION
- **Form Handler**: Contact Form 7 (Integrated with the Titan Theme).
- **Booking**: Calendly or SavvyCal (Embedded on the /book page).
- **Lead Enrichment**: Apollo.io or Hunter.io (For prospecting).
- **Tracking**: Google Analytics 4 & Meta Pixel.

### 3. OPERATIONS & PROJECT MANAGEMENT
- **Command Center**: Notion or ClickUp (To track the 14-day sprint tasks).
- **Communication**: Slack (Dedicated channels for each project).
- **Storage**: Google Drive or Dropbox (For shared client assets).
- **Video Handover**: Loom (For walkthroughs and strategy blueprints).

### 4. SALES & LEGAL
- **CRM**: Pipedrive or HubSpot (Sync leads from the Project Planner).
- **Proposals**: BetterProposals or PandaDoc.
- **Invoicing**: Stripe or Wise (Direct integration for global payments).

### 5. DESIGN & CONTENT
- **Visual Prototype**: Figma (For custom UI exploration).
- **Stock Assets**: Unsplash / Adobe Stock (For high-impact hero images).
- **AI Copywriting**: Claude or ChatGPT (As an assistant to the Blueprint).

---
*Automation is the key to velocity. If you do it twice, automate it.*
