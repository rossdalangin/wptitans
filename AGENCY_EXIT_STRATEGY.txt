TITAN AGENCY: LONG-TERM EXIT STRATEGY
======================================

PURPOSE: To build an agency that is an ASSET, not just a job.

1. THE "FOUNDER DEPENDENCY" AUDIT
- Ask: "Could the agency deliver a 14-Day Sprint if I was on vacation for a month?"
- Goal: Move from Founder-led sales/delivery to System-led sales/delivery.

2. DOCUMENTATION AS EQUITY
- Every SOP (including the Titan Master SOP) increases the valuation of the business.
- Ensure all Client Portal interactions and Pipeline metrics are stored in a central, audit-ready database.

3. RECURRING REVENUE RATIO
- Aim for 50%+ of monthly revenue to come from "Titan Maintenance" and "Insights Studio" subscriptions.
- Buyers value predictable recurring cash flow over "one-time" project spikes.

4. BRAND EQUITY
- Position the agency around the "Authority Gap" intellectual property.
- When the market associates the system (The Velocity Protocol) with the result (Authority), the agency becomes sellable to larger marketing firms or equity groups.

5. THE VALUATION TARGET
- Standard Agencies: 2x - 4x Annual EBITDA.
- Systemized High-Ticket Agencies (Titan Tier): 5x - 7x Annual EBITDA.

"Build like you're going to keep it forever, but systemize like you're selling it tomorrow."
